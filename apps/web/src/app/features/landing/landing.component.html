Why GymCrest

Retire the register and the spreadsheet.

Most gyms run on notebooks, spreadsheets and WhatsApp. GymCrest puts it all in one place.

The usual way

  • Membership dates in a register or spreadsheet
  • Chasing renewals one message at a time
  • Cash and online payments tracked in different places
  • Workout plans handed out on paper

  • Every member, plan and expiry date in one list
  • "Expiring soon" reminders sent for you
  • Every payment, invoice and receipt together
  • Plans members follow and log in their own app
For gyms

Everything your gym runs on.
Nothing it doesn't need.

Built for gym owners, front desks and trainers, with a free app for every member.

One platform

The right view for every role in your gym.

Owners, staff, trainers and members each get a portal built around their job, all working from the same live data.

  • Owners see revenue, renewals and expiring memberships at a glance.
  • Trainers build workout and diet plans with each client's history alongside.
  • Members get a focused app for workouts, meals and progress.

Members & memberships

Onboard members, sell plans and packages, and freeze or renew memberships in a couple of clicks.

Billing & payments

Members pay online through your own Razorpay account, or record cash at the desk. Invoices and receipts included.

Renewal reminders

Your own "expiring soon" and "expired" messages go out automatically, in the app and by email.

Desk check-in

Verify members at the front desk by card or search, with expired memberships flagged instantly.

Staff, trainers & branches

Invite your team with the access each role needs, assign trainers, and run several branches from one account.

Inventory & shop

Track equipment and maintenance, manage stock, and sell supplements or merchandise with emailed receipts.

For members

Your training, in your pocket. Free.

Use GymCrest on your own, or with your gym's membership linked to the same account.

  • Workout and diet plans built around your goal.
  • Log every session and meal, with nutrition lookup for your food.
  • See progress by muscle group, body measurements and health stats.
  • Your gym's announcements and membership details in one place.
