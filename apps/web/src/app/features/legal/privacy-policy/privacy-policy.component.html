GymCrest helps gyms run their business and helps people track their training, diet and progress. This policy explains what personal data GymCrest collects, why, who it is shared with and the choices you have. It is written for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules.

1. Who we are

GymCrest is operated by {{ legal.operator }}, {{ legal.location }} ("GymCrest", "we", "us"). For any question about this policy or your data, email {{ legal.contactEmail }}.

2. Two ways we handle your data

  • Your own account. When you sign up yourself to track your training, GymCrest decides how that data is used and is responsible for it.
  • Your gym's records. When a gym adds you as a member, the gym decides what to record about you (for example your plan, attendance, measurements or medical notes) and is responsible for it. GymCrest stores and processes those records on the gym's behalf. Questions or requests about them should go to your gym first; we will help where we can.

4. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in
  • To build and show workout and diet plans, track progress and log meals
  • To let your gym manage your membership, attendance, plans and billing
  • To send service emails: verification codes, password resets, invitations, receipts and plan or membership notices
  • To process payments, prevent misuse and keep the service secure
  • To fix problems and improve GymCrest

We process your data on the basis of your consent and, where the law allows, for legitimate uses such as providing a service you asked for or meeting a legal obligation. We do not sell your personal data and we do not show ads.

6. How long we keep it

  • We keep your data while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, it is permanently removed within about 24 hours. Copies in backups are cleared as those backups are routinely replaced.
  • Records that belong to a gym (such as your membership and payment history with that gym) are kept for as long as the gym keeps them, and invoices and payment records may be kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

7. Your rights

Under Indian data protection law you can:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and how it is used
  • Correct or update it (most of it is editable on your Account and Settings pages)
  • Delete your account and data (from your Account page, or by emailing us)
  • Withdraw consent at any time, which may stop some features from working
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to
  • Raise a grievance with us and, if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India

Email {{ legal.contactEmail }} to make a request. We will reply within 30 days. For data your gym entered, we may forward your request to the gym.

8. Children

People under 18 may use GymCrest only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian. Gyms that add members under 18 must obtain that consent before entering their data. We do not knowingly track children's behaviour or send them targeted advertising. If you believe a child's data was added without consent, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords are stored only as secure hashes, and access is limited to the people and services that need it. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Cookies and browser storage

GymCrest stores only what it needs in your browser: your sign-in session, saved settings (such as dark mode) and unfinished form drafts. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

11. Emails

Service emails (verification codes, password resets, invitations, receipts and plan notices) are always sent because they are needed to use your account. Optional emails from your gym, such as membership reminders, can be turned off in your account settings under Notifications.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as GymCrest changes. We will change the date at the top, and for significant changes we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect.
